    About Perla Homes

    My story began with a love of the sea. I am passionate about diving, and I know that true beauty does not always lie on the surface. This inspired me to create Perla Homes — an agency where searching for a home is like searching for your own pearl.

    I believe in quiet luxury: harmony, quality, attention to detail and trust without loud promises. I work with clients personally, to understand their needs and help them find the right home.

    What matters to me is not the number of deals, but understanding the client and their lifestyle — and finding the home that suits them.
