1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for this website is Perla Homes, an independent real estate agency in Marbella, Costa del Sol.

  • Phone and WhatsApp: +34 627 986 515
  • Location: Marbella, Costa del Sol, Spain

For privacy requests, contact us by phone or WhatsApp at +34 627 986 515.

2. What data we collect

Contact form

When you use the contact form, it asks for:

  • your name and e-mail address (required);
  • your phone number (optional);
  • your preferred language;
  • your message (optional);
  • your confirmation that you accept this policy.

The form does not send anything to our own server. When you press “Send request”, your browser opens WhatsApp with a message that contains these details. They reach us only if you send that message in WhatsApp.

When you contact us directly

If you call us or write to us on WhatsApp, we receive the details you share with us, such as your name, phone number and the content of your messages.

Technical data

Like any website, our hosting provider receives technical data needed to deliver the pages to you, such as your IP address, the date and time of the visit, the page requested and your browser type.

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis

We use the details you send us only to answer your requests about properties and to stay in touch with you about them.

  • Contact form and messages: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by ticking the box in the form and sending the request. You can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect anything we did before.
  • Technical data: our legitimate interest in running the website securely (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not make automated decisions about you.

4. Who receives your data

  • Vercel Inc. hosts this website and processes the technical data described above on our behalf. Vercel may process data outside the European Union; such transfers rely on the safeguards required by the GDPR.
  • WhatsApp (WhatsApp Ireland Limited, part of Meta) transmits your request when you choose to send it through WhatsApp. WhatsApp’s own privacy policy applies to its service.

Links to Instagram and WhatsApp open those services; nothing from them is loaded on our pages until you follow a link.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your details as long as necessary to answer your request. After that we delete them, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected (rectification);
  • have your data deleted (erasure);
  • restrict how we process your data;
  • object to the processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format (portability);
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

To use any of these rights, contact us by phone or WhatsApp at +34 627 986 515.

You also have the right to complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, AEPD): www.aepd.es.

7. Cookies and browser storage

This website does not set any cookies. It keeps a few small entries in your browser’s local storage that the site needs to work. They stay on your device and are not sent to us.

Categories

  • Necessary: the entries above. They are always on, because the site cannot work properly without them.
  • Analytics: not used.
  • Marketing: not used.

The fonts and scripts of this website are hosted on our own site. We do not load analytics, advertising, maps or other third-party content.

How to change your choice

Use “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page to see and change your choice at any time; changes apply immediately. You can also delete the entries above by clearing your browser’s site data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we start using new categories of cookies, we will ask for your choice again.
